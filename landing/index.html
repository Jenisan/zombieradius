How it works

Your neighborhood is the map

Zombie Radius uses your phone's GPS to put every player on a live radar. Everything happens on screen. The distance is real.

Open it anywhere

It runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app. Pick a name, set your home zone, and you're in.

Watch the radar

Zombies close in from the edge of the map. Get within about 50 ft and the bite is on. Shove, fight back, or run.

Survive the season

Earn points, level up and climb the board. When the season ends, points become XP, gear wipes and everyone starts over as human.

Search crates on the map for weapons, batteries, medicine and armor. Searching takes time, and time is noise.

Humans leave a trail. Zombies can follow it, and scent spray can throw them off.

Safety first

Real streets. Real rules.

Zombie Radius is built so the scary part stays on the screen. Privacy and safety are part of the rules, not an afterthought.

Read the rules in the game
Masked location

Other players only see your exact spot within about 50 ft. Further out you're a rough zone. Close the app and you leave the map.

Home stealth

Within 500 ft of home you're invisible: off the radar, out of alerts and impossible to bite.

Safety pause

One tap hides you from everyone so you can deal with what's around you. Set safe hours for overnight too.

No contact, ever

Bites, scratches and attacks only happen on screen. No following, stalking or touching other players.

No real names

Play under a character name. No real names, no photos, and block or report anyone in a tap.

Adults in the field

Real-world GPS play is for players 18 and older.

For local businesses

Become a stockpile

Sponsored stockpiles put a supply drop at your location. Players come to you to restock, so foot traffic comes with them.

FAQ

Before you head out

Do I need to download anything?

No. Zombie Radius runs in your phone's browser. For the best experience, use "Add to Home Screen" and it opens full screen like a regular app.

Is it free?

Yes. Anything you can buy later is cosmetic only. It won't make you stronger.

Can other players see where I live?

No. Set your home zone and you're hidden within 500 ft of it. Outside that, others only see your exact position when they're within about 50 ft.

What happens if I close the app to hide?

You drop off the map, but hiding has a cost. When you come back you're briefly vulnerable, and it's harsher if you bailed in the middle of a chase. Use safe hours or the safety pause for real breaks.

What happens when I get bitten?

You turn. You lose your loadout but keep any helmet and armor as extra HP, and now you hunt. A human with an antidote can bring you back.

How long is a season?

Each season runs as its own outbreak. When it ends, points turn into XP, gear and bunkers are wiped, and everyone starts human again.
