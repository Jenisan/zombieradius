ZombieRadius

The outbreak is closer than you think.

  • Zombies hold to bite humans within 50 ft and smash supply stockpiles.
  • Humans start empty-handed. Scavenge weapons, armor and gear to fight back.
  • Noise gives you away. Moving, fighting and gunfire ping your spot to zombies.

Location needed

Zombie Radius uses your location while the game is open. It's never shown exactly to anyone unless they're within 50 ft, and never while you're at home.
