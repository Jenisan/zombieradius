ZombieRadius

A real-world outbreak. Your neighborhood is the map.

  • Zombies bite humans within 50 ft. Hold to bite — the human gets a warning.
  • Humans survive with antidotes, vaccines, flares and masks. 3+ together can't be bitten.
  • Stockpiles around town hold supplies. Go get them.

Location needed

Zombie Radius uses your location while the game is open. It's never shown exactly to anyone unless they're within 50 ft, and never while you're at home.
